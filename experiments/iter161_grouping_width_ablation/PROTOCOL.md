# Iter161: separate grouping from width under a fixed transition cap

Freeze this protocol, literal CASES, PLAN, source inventory and qualification
before collecting any factorial search outcome. One acquisition; commit raw
before independent admission. No retries, outcome-driven tuning or added arms.

## Population and controls

Four disclosed development controls: bend left/right and chicane left/right.
The left fixtures are byte-identical to Iter159. Each right fixture is a complete
lateral reflection: reset, route, foreground, point population, ground and witness
commands. Absolute time, dimensions and progress checkpoints stay fixed. A separate
field-by-field implementation checks reflection. All four constructive witnesses
must complete under scalar replay before freeze. These are two paired scene
families, not four independent population samples. Historical Iter159 fixtures
and outcomes remain unchanged; its foreground placement was not a full reflection.

The planner receives geometry, route, model contract and collision-query access,
never the fixture ID or witness commands. Routes were constructed from witnesses;
this is privileged development evidence, not perception or a road benchmark.
The chicane witness brakes from 4 to 2 m/s; this does not prove braking necessary.

## Interventions

The 2×2 factorial crosses grouping (`quantized`, `exact`) with beam width (24, 48).
Order is fixed in PLAN. All arms retain Iter159 route guidance, speed-group beam
ordering, proposal order, duplicate-command removal, hard predicates, completion
definition and stopping rules. Quantized grouping uses the existing five-component
cell. Exact grouping uses the complete binary64 representation of all five state
components, preserving signed zero, plus absolute frame. No NaN or infinity.
Frame is also included in the quantized key; all candidates at a depth share it.
The score chooses one representative per key, then speed-group rounds choose
at most the width limit. Exact duplicates select lowest effort, then original ID.
The checker implements the exact key with float hex strings, independently of
the producer's packed binary representation.

Each arm has an 18,000 attempted-transition cap, 96-action horizon, 120-second
search limit and the same memory envelope. Attempts include hard rejections;
background queries are counted separately. Wider beams can reach shallower depth
under this cap. This is the width policy's total effect under the declared budget,
not a comparison at equal depth, equal actual work or equal wall time. Early
completion/empty beam stops the run. An interrupted partial depth admits no
completion and updates no beam. All 16 results, including misses, are retained.

Dynamics remain the 0.25-second five-state bicycle update. Controls, foreground
sweep, strict background count >100 and its numerical ambiguity policy, route
distance, 0.05 progress slack at absolute frames 16 and 32, and nearest-index
90%-route completion are unchanged. Completion before a checkpoint ends the path;
no later observation is invented. Background checks concern sampled states only.
The route score is a heuristic, not an admissible bound or viability certificate.

## Measurements and claims

For every completed depth record all cell representatives and the final beam.
Clear candidates partition into grouping-removed, width-removed and retained.
These are repeated temporal selection events, not distinct paths or causal
contributions to overall failure. Report partial-depth survivors separately.
The first lost constructive prefix is classified as grouping replacement, width
pruning or termination; also record whether its replacement survived the beam.
Identical-state replacement is still an identity-prefix loss, not loss of that
state's possible continuation. No new fixed-suffix rollout study is included.

Report each case's completion, stop, actual attempts/queries, completed depth,
selected-prefix progress, both removal counts and first witness loss. Report
grouping contrasts at each width, width contrasts for each grouping, and the
difference of those differences. Completion contrasts are paired binary changes
on these controls; no significance tests or population claims. They include all
downstream search consequences, not just one local grouping decision.

Primary claim: exact state grouping at width 24 completes all four controls.
All four independently admitted completions support it. Any admitted miss
contradicts it. A missing run, identity mismatch, checker failure or resource
violation makes the study invalid, not a search miss. Do not rescue the candidate
by reporting width 48 or changing the denominator after seeing evidence.

The independent checker reconstructs dynamics, controls, foreground geometry and
sweep, route, progress, every full background mask and completion for four
witnesses and 16 selected paths. It replays both selection stages from retained
producer scores and clear labels, with full source/raw binding and inventory.
It does not independently recompute every unselected hard rejection or guide score.
No native recovery, information advantage, physical safety, continuous background
clearance, road compliance, transfer or superiority claim follows.

## Resources and acceptance

Local CPU only. Producer <=2,400 seconds, checker <=1,800 seconds, each <=1 GiB
peak RSS, raw <=256 MiB, >=2 GiB free before acquisition. Setup, witness validation,
trace materialization and scalar admission are separate measured costs. One
initialization; preserve partial evidence on failure. No provider, GPU or spend.
Acceptance requires the frozen identity chain, all 16 records, all four valid
witnesses, complete scalar selected-path admission and mechanical retention replay.
Operational acceptance of an adverse result does not qualify the candidate.
