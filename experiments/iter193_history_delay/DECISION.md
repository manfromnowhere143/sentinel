# Preserve the integrated gain; do not promote it to intervention recovery

Iter192 demonstrates a finite integrated improvement. Iter193 does not establish
complete recovery from actual history: 0/6 returned completions, four censored
searches, and two bounded misses. Its primary conclusion is inconclusive, not
physical infeasibility. The valid 9.75-second partial path is a distinct result.

The actual first-fire no-delay run invokes feedback and builds a substantially
advancing retained tree, then expires. Two delayed states instead meet the
transition reservation boundary. This separates observed stopping mechanisms;
it does not isolate why a complete solution was not found. The separate exploratory
replay now admits native node 44 from the exact no-delay first-fire history:
38 new controls reach 15.75 seconds and progress 0.63986510, with both original
checkpoints passed. This is a checked partial continuation, not a complete recovery
or an online return. Unexpanded-node counts alone establish neither mechanism
failure nor further feasible completion.

The next decisive action is a **separately preregistered, matched-history comparison
with a fixed conventional feedback-policy library**, qualified for braking,
stationary steering, obstacle departure and route continuation on constructed
controls before source execution. Charge all rollouts, scene predicates, setup and
wall time; keep original deadlines and action limits. Compare complete checked
witnesses, deadline misses, partial progress and harm, not just proposal counts.
Use the admitted partial continuation as a replay/representation control, not a
hidden target or privileged winning seed for one method.

Measure dense-scene query cost and other execution stages before claiming a
bottleneck. Any acceleration must first demonstrate exact predicate parity on
retained queries and adversarial geometry; it must be available to comparators.
Do not combine such a change with a new allocation rule or propose a speedup from
the wall/query correlation alone. Four-case tuning stops; these six source rows
must not become an unbounded repair loop.

A complete source witness must then survive native execution. Only after that
gate should a locked unseen-case intervention study compare the frozen planner,
existing braking monitor, accessible released learned monitor and qualified
predictive safety filter/MPC under matched information and compute. Unavailable
published artifacts are unavailable, not permission to substitute a weak proxy.
The [comparison design](../../docs/research/SENTINEL_NEXT_COMPARISON_AND_PRODUCT_GATE_2026-10-28.md)
records the qualification and population rules. That study has not run.

Choose **offline recovery qualification as the first product hypothesis**, with
checked witnesses, justified exclusions and explicit unknowns. Its customer value
and cost advantage still require evidence. Runtime supervision remains
unqualified: searches took 37.5–120.0 seconds, all full workers exceeded a 250 ms
tick, and no complete native intervention was executed. No superiority, valuation
or acquisition-readiness conclusion follows from the present results.
