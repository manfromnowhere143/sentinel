# Decision before evidence

Iter179 establishes three checkpoint continuations, two progress exclusions and
eleven unresolved misses, but its witnesses used 495–510 transitions. Two viable
roots received no original outgoing request. Neither observation establishes a
repair within the integrated connector's 128-transition allowance. Keep the
correction: the retained generator already includes changing, multi-phase controls.

Proceed with the fixed paired comparison after constructed controls and automated
source/runtime/input checks. Use the complete root population. The candidate width
comes from a worst-case work bound, not a sweep over retained outcomes. The new
common target and checkpoint objective must remain explicit. All queried states
receive scalar replay; exact disagreement invalidates admission.

A gain with no loss supports testing one later integrated substitution under the
existing total budgets. A local success is not an integrated qualification: root
selection, horizon/target choice and returning a prefix can still prevent its use.
A loss rejects an unconditional replacement. Equal misses leave the diagnosis
unresolved and do not establish that more compute is necessary. If the retained
program arm already finds useful continuations within 128, investigate integrated
allocation/selection before adding another local control mechanism. Preserve every
outcome, and close this comparison without a cap, width or seed sweep.

All four integrated cases must qualify before source comparison. Actual-history
intervention, an advancing environment during measured delay and a responsive
comparator remain required for the mission. No claim of frontier superiority is
supported by a finite diagnostic improvement alone.
