# Conditional opportunity and its limits

For each accepted endpoint let j be its independently reconstructed nearest-route
index. Define J as their maximum. The audit population contains each eligible
trial prefix with index strictly greater than J, preserving record identity
(connection, evaluation, prefix) even if two records share a numerical state.
Within each connection order by (-j, absolute frame, evaluation, prefix) and
probe the first element. J=117 is a retained descriptive value, not a tuned threshold.

For root r found during connection c, let W(c) be the sum of transition attempts
through the entire connection, including unselected and rejected programs.
The conditional work ceiling is B(r)=18,000-W(c). Do not charge only the selected
path. Let T be the complete original case search wall. The conditional search
deadline is 120-T; sampled target time remains uniform on [0,96], while physical
evolution begins at r's retained absolute frame. Each root gets its own conditional
accounting; these are three alternative counterfactual diagnostics, not one pooled
18,000-transition integrated run.

A witness consists of a checked complete reset-to-root prefix and a checked
root-to-completion suffix. Exact join state and absolute time are required.
All original dynamics, collision masks, route thresholds, checkpoints and terminal
rules hold on the joined trajectory. Fresh conditional native search is not the
causal effect of inserting the root into the prior tree.

Timing exclusions reuse [Iter182's declared numerical domain](../iter182_late_frontier_timing/MATHEMATICS.md),
with separate rational corner checkers. They apply only at frames 24–31 here.
No exclusion outside that domain means unknown. A scalar-admitted prefix alone
does not imply an admissible continuation. Neither a finite-search miss nor a
failure to find a certificate establishes impossibility.

Scalar masks cover every queried state in each conditional acquisition, including
alternatives discarded by selection. This checks numerical source-model labels;
physical scene fidelity, continuous inter-frame background collision, sensing,
real-time actuation and population generalization remain outside the claim.
