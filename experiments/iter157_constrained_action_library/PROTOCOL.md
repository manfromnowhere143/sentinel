# Iter157: complete constraints on the retained finite action library

12 October 2026. Prospective development diagnostic; no new background outcomes
from this library have been examined when this procedure is registered. The same
75 Iter153 feedback-control specifications and their 150 paired model traces are
already exposed. Iter153 admitted 14 under its foreground, control and progress
conditions; Iter154 then invalidated the selected maneuver through background
collision. Iter156 qualified a complete source background query. This study adds
that missing condition without inventing a new library after inspecting results.

Question: does any member of this exact finite library still qualify after the
source background predicate is required at reset and every retained 0.25-second
post-action state? This is an offline feasibility result on one exposed scene,
not an intervention outcome, physical-clearance proof, continuous background sweep,
responsive-policy superiority result or information-benefit experiment.

The 75 specifications, dynamics, feedback policy, 401-action maximum horizon,
foreground swept-margin test, actuator/yaw/lateral-acceleration bounds, source route
completion and progress at both recorded hazard times remain exactly Iter153's.
Progress may lag either retained comparator by no more than 0.05 source route
completion. The source completes near 90% of route samples; this is not literal
route-end arrival. The nominal and geometric-monitor comparators are historical;
they are not a newly reproduced competent responsive repair policy.

All 75 candidates remain in the denominator. The 61 that failed prior conditions
retain those reasons; their new background condition is unevaluated, never zero.
For each of the 14 previously eligible candidates, scan its first model replica
from reset until its first collision, numerical ambiguity or source completion.
The second replica is byte-checked for trajectory agreement, not counted as an
independent scene. Every query uses all 276,519 original selected centers; retain
full packed masks, counts, numeric diagnostics, boxes, ground index and timings.
No ROI thinning, threshold change or post-result action modification is allowed.
A source collision is strict count >100. Diagnostic threshold-straddling abstains.
The roundoff band is diagnostic, not a certified floating-point bound.

Eligibility requires every prior condition and a clear background result at every
sample through completion. Rank qualified candidates by completion time, absolute
reference offset and lexical ID, exactly the prior ranking. Also report a fixed
minimal-deviation selection (absolute offset, completion time, ID) on the identical

end-to-end systems. Reproduce the failed maneuver_059 as a mandatory negative
control: no earlier reported rejection, 273 centers at frame 13 / 3.25 seconds.

Freeze source and this plan before one exclusive producer. Commit all raw before
a separate checker. That checker replays every prior control/dynamics/foreground/
progress trace with Iter153's separate scalar implementation, reconstructs the
complete Iter155 selected population, and checks a full scalar background mask

candidate. It also checks every queried negative-control sample. It does not
import the vector query or producer. Earlier clear samples of other rejected
candidates remain producer diagnostics; their first-rejection timing is not an
independently established earliest time. One scalar rejection witness is enough
to exclude a candidate. Retain the exact coverage rather than implying all raw
queries received scalar replay.

New gates require corruption controls: missing/duplicate candidate, changed input,
partial/nonconsecutive trajectory, favorable count/mask edit, ambiguity treated as
clear, dropped failure, altered ranking, wrong raw/source commit, source drift,
missing mask and exceeded resources. Invalid or missing evidence stops admission.
One producer attempt; no retry of initialized output. Invalid and partial files
remain. No data-dependent library expansion or new simulator episode is permitted.

seconds, 1 GiB measured peak RSS per process and 64 MiB raw derivatives. At least
256 MiB free space is required. Native arrays and raw files have bounded reads.
No provider, GPU, container, credentials or new cloud spending. Outer process
timeouts bound a stuck computation; no real-time guarantee follows from timing.
