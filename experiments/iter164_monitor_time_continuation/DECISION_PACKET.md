# Test the state where the monitor actually intervenes

14 October 2026. Iter163 admits two native completions from reset, but that path
changes steering at 0.25 seconds. The retained monitor first fires at 6.25 seconds.
Whether a useful action remains available after that history is the consequential
scientific bottleneck. Neither a new learned model nor another reset-time rerun
answers it.

The next milestone is one independently checked source-model continuation after
preserving all 25 earlier controls. First qualify both historical prefixes against
complete geometry and absolute time. Then run the already qualified exact/24 search
once under its existing per-search budget. This keeps method, constraints and
stopping stable while changing the question to the actual intervention state.

The falsifier is a valid bounded miss from an eligible prefix. If the prefix itself
fails a retained hard constraint, retain that discrepancy and leave continuation
uninitialized. A failure of admission or resources is invalid evidence. A finite
search result cannot decide global controllability.

Alternatives have different costs. A larger beam previously lost completions under
a fixed attempt cap. A learned propagator introduces a calibration problem before
resolving the known timing/state contract. A released planner is a valuable next
comparator, but its state-dependent proposals, predecessor-dependent sweep and exact
completion rule need qualification. The [source review](RESEARCH.md) keeps those
alternatives visible without claiming an unperformed benchmark.

Required resources are local CPU, retained public research records and at least
4 GiB free disk. The prefix producer/checker budgets are 300/600 seconds; the single
continuation producer/checker budgets are 2,400/1,800 seconds. Every process stays
within 1 GiB peak RSS and outputs within 256 MiB. No provider, GPU or spend is needed.
[Protocol](PROTOCOL.md) and [plan](PLAN.json) fix acceptance, missingness and stopping.

Acceptance requires both independently qualified prefixes, one complete admitted
suffix under the original absolute deadlines, full scalar geometry replay of the
concatenated path, and retained failure/resource accounting. Even acceptance leaves
native continuation, computation latency, useful information and transfer unresolved.
