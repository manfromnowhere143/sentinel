# Separate available programs, selected prefixes and tree requests

Iter175 spends the same maximum budget with shorter connections and improves
completion from 1/4 to 2/4. Both right-hand cases miss, with different seeds and
different trees from their mirrored counterparts. No eight-step solve reaches
Halton. Four discarded chicane_right prefixes reach frame 32 but have low route
progress and lack full scalar-mask admission. Those observations identify
questions; they do not identify a working repair.

The smallest useful next task checks nominal reflected program availability on
every retained executed request and scalar-admits the four specifically identified
partial paths with their ancestors. No cap sweep, source comparison or additional
integrated outcome is acquired. This audit is exploratory after exposed results.
Its primary property and all interpretation limits are in PROTOCOL.md.

The constructed controls already show why a blanket directional explanation is
unsafe: a full steering-pair prefix can preserve coverage, while an initial cache
duplicate can move the same transition cutoff into an incomplete pair. Exact
finite set closure also differs from distributional symmetry and from pathwise
symmetry with coupled random inputs. Quantify the retained population instead of
declaring either a directional bug or a symmetry guarantee.

After this audit, prefer a mechanism whose control behavior addresses a measured
limitation. Target-conditioned local guidance, prefix selection and exploration
coverage are distinct alternatives. Increasing a cap again would confound the
allocation result already closed. A failure to reach the next checkpoint in any
proposed trajectory is different from discarding an already-valid continuation.
Longer time with lower progress does not automatically favor a new ranking.

The broader objective remains a usable action before its deadline from actual
executed histories, followed by a controlled information comparison against a
responsive baseline. The existing beam qualification and all independent planner
failures remain visible. Direct operator authority supplies operational approval;
hashes, scalar replay and known-bads supply bounded evidence, not human endorsement.
