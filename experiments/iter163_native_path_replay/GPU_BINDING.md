# Native binding

Use the unchanged retained runtime and asset inventory from Iter145, the original
scene/scenario, existing simulator environment and L4 device. Recompute hashes of
all bound files and aliases during preparation and postflight. No package, asset,
source or machine-shape substitution is authorized by this protocol.

The Iter154 mechanisms are copied into a separately versioned Iter163 namespace
to bind the new 66-action packet, episode identities and background telemetry.
Historical source and results remain unchanged. Reused observation reconciliation,
provider identity, timed actor, foreground and independent motion implementations
retain their original namespaces and are explicitly inventoried in INPUTS.json.
The producer's source mask tap changes instrumentation only. Its retained AST
identity and threshold/known-bad fixtures are prerequisites to native initialization.

Read [HYPOTHESIS.md](HYPOTHESIS.md), [STUDY_PLAN.json](STUDY_PLAN.json) and
[DECISION_PACKET.md](DECISION_PACKET.md) for scope and acceptance. Source-method CPU
fixtures cannot substitute for Linux ownership qualification, native GPU renders,
real actor transforms or observed provider closure.
