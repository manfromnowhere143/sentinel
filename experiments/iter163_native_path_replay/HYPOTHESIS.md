## Stopping, missingness and interpretation

Both replicas are attempted even after a valid collision or other scientific
failure. Infrastructure failure, changed identity, resource stop, unparseable or
missing telemetry stops the pair and makes complete qualification invalid. Preserve
all initialized, partial and uninitialized slots. No restart of an initialized slot.

A native collision/off-route event or material background/model discrepancy is a
contradicted qualification. Two matching, independently admitted completions with
both checkpoints and supported background replay support the bounded hypothesis.
Otherwise valid horizon exhaustion, numerical ambiguity or replica disagreement
is inconclusive. A partial runner cannot support completion. Native termination
takes precedence over unused packet actions. Successful reset-time execution does
not establish recovery from the actual monitor history.
