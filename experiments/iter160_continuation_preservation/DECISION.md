# Isolate retention without mistaking a small terminal difference for the cause

12 October 2026. Iter160 establishes one local same-command completion difference.
It does not diagnose the candidate's failure. The route/speed-group candidate's
first retained representative still admits the complete witness suffix. Changing
that first replacement alone is therefore not supported as the repair for its
miss. The endpoint pair's failure is also narrow: one nearest-route sample short
of the completion criterion, with every observed hard constraint passing.
Do not relabel it as collision, loss of all feasible continuations or a safety result.

A [post-result log audit](POST_RESULT_RETENTION.md) finds that four initial cell
winners were not retained by the final beam. For all twelve retained bindings,
the next child of the tested continuation is replaced again. This locates later
selection events without establishing their whole-search effect. It strengthens
the need to distinguish approximate cells from the separate width cap.

The next milestone remains one **fixed-budget retention ablation**, with a
limit on how much bespoke planner work Sentinel should absorb. Compare the
current quantized-cell rule against an exact full-state-and-frame key, holding
proposal generation, guide, width, expansion cap, hard predicates and stopping
rules fixed. Exact aggregation means binary64 state identity and absolute frame;
retain the lowest-effort representative when those keys are identical. This
isolates approximate aggregation but leaves beam-width loss possible. It is
neither a completeness guarantee nor a qualified replacement before the test.

The same prospective study should audit the witness continuation's later losses:
check whether a surviving node's fixed suffix remains admissible only in a
separately labeled diagnostic replay, never by giving the search an oracle.
Report lost opportunity alongside actual completions and attempted transitions.
If approximate aggregation is removed but the candidate still misses a known
feasible case, do not answer by adding another untested score or raising the cap.
The resulting guide/width interaction or inadequate proposal ordering needs a
separate explanation, or a released planner adapter should replace further
bespoke baseline development.

Preserve the old four-case outcomes. Before acquisition, produce a new version of
full-scene reflections, including foreground objects, route, reset, witness
commands, ground and point population. Test the complete transform and round-trip
identity. The existing cases can remain exposed diagnostic controls; corrected
reflections are new controls, not a repair to historical evidence. Register all
arms and cases before outcomes and retain unsuccessful searches. Acceptance
still requires completion of every registered known-feasible control under
independent full scalar admission and a declared resource envelope.

The released alternative is OMPL control::SST or a more suitable qualified
kinodynamic adapter. Its sampling, propagation, timed progress and exact collision
predicate must be mapped explicitly; default configuration is not a competent
comparison by assertion. Compare adapter effort with one remaining isolated
retention experiment, not an open-ended competition to invent a planner.

No source-scene or native run is justified by Iter160. The research dependency
is still a competent, admissible action baseline. Native useful recovery,
actual monitor decision-time history, an equal-cost responsive information
ablation, and held-out transfer remain the mission. Iter135 stays deferred.
