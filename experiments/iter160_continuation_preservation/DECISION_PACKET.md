# Test the explanation before changing the planner

12 October 2026. Iter159's proposed route/speed-group search completed three of
four known-feasible controls. The older endpoint/speed-group arm also completed
three. Every exact witness prefix was replaced inside a state cell, but eleven
of sixteen searches still completed. First loss is evidence of a replacement,
not an explanation of every failed search.

The consequential bottleneck remains an independently admissible, useful action
baseline for Sentinel's recovery and information experiments. Native execution
cannot resolve a search defect that already appears on known-feasible models.
More elaborate ranking has not demonstrated better aggregate coverage.

The recommended milestone is ten fixed continuations from five paired states.
Their actual histories and absolute time are preserved. Falsification is direct:
a representative rejects or fails to complete under the commands that complete
from the displaced witness. Independent scalar replay must admit that difference.
If all ten complete, reject this first-replacement explanation and investigate
later retention/width interactions rather than claiming a diagnosed cell defect.

Alternatives are another guide, a wider search, a smaller cell, or qualifying a
released planner adapter. The first three change search behavior before the
current explanation has been tested. A released implementation remains a serious
option; its dynamics, timing and collision interface need qualification. This
bounded diagnostic is cheaper and identifies whether there is a local effect
worth isolating in a subsequent aggregation ablation.

Primary-source review was refreshed before acquisition:

- [Moncton and Frew, 2609.04464v1, 3 September 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.04464v1)
  distinguishes retaining a reference-like trajectory from retaining alternatives.
  Its SST result assumes dynamic clearance and regularity not established here.
  Losing the witness therefore does not license a causal or completeness claim.
- [OMPL control::SST documentation](https://ompl.kavrakilab.org/core/classompl_1_1control_1_1SST.html)
  exposes selection and pruning radii and their narrow-passage tradeoffs. It is a
  candidate released baseline, not a qualified replacement for this contract.
- [SMO-RRT/SMO-SST, 2607.19284v1, 21 July 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.19284v1)
  separates completeness from pruning and notes that expected-state projection
  can omit distribution/adversary state. Future belief/history aggregation needs
  its own sufficiency argument. This deterministic test proves none of that.

The acceptance criteria, ten-record denominator, stopping/missingness rules and
CPU/memory/disk limits are frozen in PROTOCOL and PLAN. Scientific implementations
are separate; one agent may produce and operationally validate them without
misrepresenting that as independent human review. No external resource is required.

After the result, choose one isolated aggregation/retention experiment or qualify
a released adapter. Do not turn Sentinel into an indefinite custom-planner
project. Its sequence remains useful source-model action, native recovery,
actual decision-time history, equal-cost responsive information comparison and
held-out transfer. No superiority, physical safety or information advantage has
been established by these synthetic controls.
