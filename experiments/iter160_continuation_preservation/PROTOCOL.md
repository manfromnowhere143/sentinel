before acquisition. One local CPU acquisition, no retries or post-outcome repair.
Commit all raw before a separately implemented scalar admission. No source-scene
search, native driving, GPU, provider or spending. Historical Iter159 bytes stay
unchanged, including its disclosed incomplete foreground reflection controls.

Population: all 16 admitted first cell replacements from Iter159, deduplicated
into five distinct case/time/state pairs. All bindings and actual prefix commands
are retained. Identical pairs have identical histories; validate that assertion
against all 16 committed attempts logs. These are five fixed diagnostics, not
independent population samples. There are four exposed constructed cases; two
pairs share chicane_left. The witness is privileged diagnostic input, not a path
found by a planner in this experiment.

For each pair, run discarded first, representative second, in INPUTS order. Each
trajectory starts at the original reset and executes its actual ancestry. At the
recorded replacement frame, append exactly the remaining commands of the admitted
witness, ending at that witness's first complete state. Do not restart the clock,
teleport, adapt commands, add time, search alternatives or tune the scene. Stop
at the first rejection, first completion, or end of that fixed suffix.

Retain every issued action and state, route outcome, predicate vector, first
failure and every queried full background mask. Evaluate in order: controls
(except reset), foreground contact, swept foreground (except reset), route distance,
timed progress when due, background, completion. Later predicates after rejection
are null. Non-due progress and reset controls/sweep are null, not observed passes.
Route measurements are computed at every recorded state, even if an earlier
predicate rejects it. Completion requires every preceding predicate to pass.

The inherited 0.25 s bicycle update, limits, swept foreground margins, nearest-index
route progress and 0.05 checkpoint slack remain unchanged. Background uses the

Independent admission reconstructs dynamics using scalar formulas, rectangle

limited to content binding and historical ancestry, not scientific evaluation.

All five discarded-state positive controls must complete. A failed control,
missing record, source drift, disagreement or resource breach invalidates the
study; never classify an admission failure as evidence against preservation.
Primary claim: every representative also completes with the fixed suffix.
Five completions support that bounded claim; any admitted non-completion
contradicts it. Report every pair and precise failure type. No p-values,
independence assumption or population extrapolation. A differing outcome proves
a local same-command distinction only; it does not prove that the representative
has no alternative continuation or that this replacement caused the entire
search to fail. All-preserved evidence rules out this first replacement alone
as a loss of the tested feasible continuation, not later losses or interactions.
