# Establish an action before attributing information value

12 October 2026. Decision made before the Iter158 search.

Sentinel has demonstrated source-level replay and a complete background predicate
on the retained scene. Iter157 independently rejected all fourteen previously
eligible actions; the fixed library contains no qualified action among 75. The
remaining 61 fail earlier conditions. These are meaningful evaluator findings,
but useful native recovery and incremental information value remain unestablished.
Iter135 remains deferred; this does not reopen its evidence or protocol.

The consequential bottleneck is an admissible intervention opportunity. The old
controller tracks one fixed quintic offset at one fixed target speed. Its null
does not establish that the source task is infeasible. A more flexible search can
also fail because of pruning, inadequate anticipation, or the source predicate.

| Alternative | Decision |
|---|---|
| Rerun the rejected native maneuver | Reject: a retained counterexample already answers it. |
| Add a learned planner or world model | Defer: training, uncertainty and interface changes would obscure the immediate question. |
| Integrate CommonRoad's full planner | Keep as a later baseline; its road model and dynamics require a real adapter and qualification. |
| Bounded search over executable commands | Choose: reuses qualified dynamics and constraints, produces a checkable trajectory or an explicit incomplete search. |
| Change the development scene | Reserve if this bounded attempt finds no opportunity; disclose selection and retain the original case. |

Milestone: construct and independently replay one full source-model trajectory
under the unchanged foreground, background, actuator and progress conditions.
Iter158 adds a deterministic beam search over acceleration/steering-rate commands
and three nominal tracking proposals. Hard constraints precede ranking. Beam
pruning is heuristic; this is neither a complete planner nor a novelty claim.

Pre-acquisition qualification rejected immediate-progress ranking on a constructed
20 m route with a 1 m obstacle at 7 m. Foreground visibility guidance alone also
failed. Adding heading alignment and preserving speed groups produced a complete,
independently replayed detour. These are disclosed synthetic development choices;
no Iter158 source-scene outcome was acquired while making them.

The experiment can falsify the inference that the fixed-library null means no
model action exists: one admitted completion is a constructive counterexample.
It cannot prove infeasibility. No completion, exhausted beam or exhausted budget
is **inconclusive** about task feasibility. An independent replay disagreement
invalidates admission; it does not trigger an unrecorded repair and rerun.

Resources: one local CPU producer, at most 1,800 seconds and 60,000 background
queries; one scalar admission, at most 1,800 seconds; 1 GiB process RSS and
128 MiB retained raw output. No provider, GPU, native drive, training or new data.
Acceptance requires the full selected trajectory, every background sample and
all foreground/control/progress conditions to pass separate scalar replay.
The best partial trajectory remains a partial trajectory, regardless of progress.

The wider sequence is unchanged: qualify a model action; test it natively;
reproduce the actual monitor trigger and available history; compare information
access against a competent responsive baseline at equal total cost; then test
held-out cases and another planner. No superiority or physical safety follows
from completing this engineering slice.

## Decision-relevant primary research

Retrieved 12 October 2026. URLs and content identities are retained in SOURCES.json.

- [CommonRoad 2024 competition report, 2512.19564v1, 22 December 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.19564v1): feasibility precedes trajectory cost. Its comparison separates coverage from quality on jointly solved cases under fixed resources. This supports separate acceptance and ranking; its results are not Sentinel evidence.
- [Released CommonRoad reactive planner](https://github.com/CommonRoad/commonroad-reactive-planner) and [drivability checker 2025.4.0](https://cps.pages.gitlab.lrz.de/commonroad-drivability-checker/api_feasibility.html): inspectable kinematic, collision, goal and boundary checks. Sentinel lacks a qualified road-boundary representation and must preserve that unknown.
- [Risk-aware motion primitives, 2607.26802v1, 29 July 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.26802v1): combines learned candidate generation, probabilistic risk assessment and MPC refinement. Its reported collisions remain nonzero. More elaborate architecture is not a reason to import an unqualified risk distribution or training dependency into this diagnostic.
- [ECO, 2609.31383v1, 25 September 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.31383v1): repairs intermediate waypoints around a retained endpoint and executed history. This is relevant to a later responsive baseline; smoothness and endpoint preservation do not establish Sentinel's full-scene admissibility.

No external method is reproduced or defeated here. The chosen experiment is a
small, falsifiable engineering step toward Sentinel's broader mission.
