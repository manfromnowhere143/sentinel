# Separate continuation feasibility from search allocation

The uniform timing amendment is locally qualified and its integrated all-four
hypothesis is contradicted. Keep the result as 3/4, with no gains or losses against
Iter187 and the same gain/loss exchange against Iter183. Do not increase the
budget, retune on the missed left case, or count later tree time as completion.
Necessary eligibility and sufficient search effort are different questions.

The admitted left tree now reaches the late window. Three registered frame-26
nodes receive no outgoing request. Their low speeds make controlled waiting a
concrete hypothesis, not an admitted witness: speed alone says nothing sufficient
about scene constraints, route progress, steering, the final suffix or remaining
work. The node that does receive requests advances only to frame 26.

The next bounded task is a **prospective continuation-and-expansion audit of every
registered frame-24-to-31 endpoint across all four Iter189 cases**, not only the
three attractive left nodes. The complete descriptive population has 22 records;
freeze their exact native identities, case/state/frame, full ancestries and source
bindings before evaluating any new suffix. Preserve repeated nodes as records;
only deduplicate compute under an exact case/state/frame identity with explicit
mapping. Include retained complete paths as false-exclusion controls.

The audit must answer three separable questions:

1. Does a physically admissible, collision-checked continuation reach the required
   frame-32 checkpoint? Qualify a fixed stop/wait/controlled-progress family and an
   already specified bounded search comparator before acquiring outcomes. A failed
   finite family is a bounded miss, never an impossibility proof.
2. Does a checkpoint witness also admit full route completion under the original
   constraints and remaining work? Record the two endpoints separately. Charge all
   diagnostic work explicitly; distinguish a separate diagnostic envelope from a
   continuation that fits the original per-node remaining transition and wall budget.
3. Was the successful continuation already generated and discarded, never proposed,
   or never requested from that node? Replay the original outgoing evaluations and
   selection records. Missing outgoing work is an observation, not proof of cause.

Use separate physical and full scalar-mask replay for every queried state, including
rejected and unselected proposals. Necessary timing bounds may classify exclusions;
unknown and censored probes remain explicit. Freeze populations, action schedules,
remaining-budget accounting, stopping and known-bads before the next acquisition.
No continuation audit earns integrated credit. An expansion or proposal amendment
requires a later unchanged-budget all-four trial, retaining every lost success.

The source-comparison gate remains closed. After qualification, the consequential
experiment preserves actual first-fire history and measures observation availability,
decision, computation and applied-action times. Information advantage then needs a
strong responsive baseline with equal observations and action limits, plus newly
held-out scenes. The [primary research review](RESEARCH.md) motivates that separation;
it does not establish parity or superiority against Tesla, Mobileye, or any other
system. No competitor comparison was run in Iter189.
