# Fundamental question: can necessary feasibility improve the complete search?

A locally correct exclusion can still reduce useful exploration after changing a
finite tree. Iter188 established two early impossibility certificates; it did not
establish a successful intervention on search. Iter189 therefore holds all original
resources fixed, charges the certificate cost and tests all cases with explicit losses.

The one operational change is a shared eligible-prefix ceiling: preserve the old
late constraints, then apply the necessary one-step rule before either selector.
Unknowns survive. A certificate cannot select a favorable known call or relax
physical predicates. Independent replay must connect a computed exclusion to actual
primary/extra/native choices, not merely check an isolated mathematical function.

All-four success is a gate to source comparison, not the final mission. The deeper
questions remain whether an action is recoverable at the actual decision time,
whether available information helps choose it within an end-to-end deadline, and
whether the benefit survives stronger responsive comparators and untouched scenes.
