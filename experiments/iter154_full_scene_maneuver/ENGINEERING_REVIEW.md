of its limits. A more elaborate detector cannot compensate for an ineffective intervention.

The HUGSIM source and previous Sentinel traces are retained, bounded and replayed. New control
qualification includes actual source methods with explicitly synthetic rendering/background
fixtures. Those fixtures do not validate the GPU or background collision implementation. Live
background evidence consists of source-bound calls on native scene points and separately checked
receipt consistency; the point cloud is not replayed locally. Preserve that limitation prominently.

Resource decisions use the retained official Google Cloud same-day price receipt, with its
original retrieval timestamp, and a conservative $3/hour upper planning envelope. Storage and
taxes remain outside the compute estimate. The website's large page could not be parsed through
the browser tool in this session; no new live price or invoice assertion is made.

A successful privileged maneuver establishes neither Sentinel's information contribution nor
useful scene transfer. The next design must separate control competence, available observations,
additional signals, latency and progress costs, with new scenes reserved before generalization.
