# Recovery comparison and product decision gate

28 October 2026. Status: proposed successor design, not a preregistered benchmark
or an executed comparison. Read the actual [Iter192 result](../../experiments/iter192_integrated_feedback/RESULT.md)
and the separately frozen [Iter193 protocol](../../experiments/iter193_history_delay/PROTOCOL.md).
This document neither changes those experiments nor declares their unexecuted
successors successful.

## What the next comparison must isolate

The four-case integration establishes that the qualified state-feedback proposal
can improve the complete bounded search on exposed inputs. It does not isolate
branch retention, establish useful intervention at first fire, or compare with a
competent predictive safety filter. Further tuning on those four cases stops.

Before a large unseen-case efficacy study, identify an independently checked,
history-preserving complete continuation and validate its native execution, or
produce a justified exclusion under explicit model assumptions. If neither is
available, retain `unknown`: another finite search failure cannot provide a
negative recovery label. Iter193's predefined failure path is a bounded diagnosis
and falsification, not a larger benchmark around an unestablished intervention.

| Comparator | Necessary qualification | Information and cost rule |
|---|---|---|
| Frozen planner, no intervention | Exact requested/applied control and timestamp replay | Same observations, scene seeds and end-to-end clock |
| Existing monitor and braking | Preserve frontal-collision regressions, release semantics and false interventions | Do not erase its known harms or substitute a different actuator |
| Simple feedback-policy library | Constructed braking, stationary steering, waiting, release and obstacle departure | Fixed policy inventory; charge every rollout and geometry query |
| Same proposal family without retention | Positive native transport controls; no hidden extra search | Same total budgets; separately expose the effect of changed used work |
| Retention without the feedback proposal | Original policy and selection contracts | Separate proposal coverage from branch retention with a declared factorial design |
| Predictive safety filter / MPC with backup | Feasible backup and terminal contract on constructed controls; test deadline and infeasibility handling | Same state/prediction access, control bounds, geometry checks and hardware; charge solver setup and all propagation |
| Released learned monitor | Accessible model/code, required planner features, compatible nominal planner and calibration population | No synthetic substitute labeled as the published method; equal access or explicit separate information strata |

The closest research families and limitations are recorded in the
[forensic review](SENTINEL_FORENSIC_FRONTIER_REVIEW_2026-10-28.md) and its
[primary-source ledger](forensic_2026_10_28/sources.json). The proposed competitors
are mechanism families, not implemented or beaten baselines. Predictive filtering
needs declared prediction/error assumptions; an optimizer returning a path is not
automatically a robust or recursively feasible certificate.

Freeze development and unseen populations, scenario identities, seeds, interventions,
comparators, shared observations, action interface, resource accounting, failures,
retries and analysis before observing unseen outcomes. Previously inspected source
scenes and the six Iter193 conditions are not holdouts. If assets or a baseline's
required features are unavailable, record it as unavailable rather than silently
replacing it with a weak proxy.

Primary outputs must include collisions and near collisions, complete useful
progress, intervention burden and harms, full response latency and deadline misses,
invalid proposals, memory, model uncertainty, and paired losses. Label a partial
valid trajectory separately from complete recovery. Preserve simulation-time delay,
solver wall time and applied-action timestamps as different quantities. A study of
offline model witnesses cannot establish simulator or physical intervention value.

## Product selection rule

The immediate product hypothesis is an **offline recovery qualification instrument**:
it binds a planner failure to actual history, searches under a declared budget,
replays returned controls, and exports a checked witness, a justified model-scoped
exclusion, or an explicit unknown. The existing research apparatus supports parts
of this workflow. Customer value, integration savings and label improvements over
competent existing methods still require measurement.

A runtime supervisor is a later hypothesis. It requires an executable intervention
before the response deadline, valid handling of uncertain or stale state, native
execution, robust transfer, and acceptable false interventions. A 120-second
offline search allowance is not evidence for a 250-millisecond control response.
Faster hardware or a learned proposal may help, but neither is credited without
measurement.

Commercial qualification should use a concrete customer task and insertion point:
for example, reduce the cost of distinguishing an avoidable planner failure from
an unresolved search miss, while retaining reproducible evidence. Measure analyst
time, compute cost, false labels and integration burden against the customer's
current process. Reproducibility alone is not a proprietary advantage; the method
must add a capability or reduce a meaningful cost. No willingness-to-pay,
acquisition-interest, defensibility or valuation claim follows from these trials.
