# Research direction against current primary evidence

Reviewed 16 October 2026. This is a bounded assessment of the sources below,
not an exhaustive literature survey or a claim that Sentinel is state of the art.
The verdict is **a relevant research question with an unproven intervention system**.
Recent progress is concrete: a discarded prefix now has a verified full-route
continuation. The missing result remains useful action from actual decision
history under measured delay, against a competent responsive comparator.

| Primary source and version/date | Evidence relevant to Sentinel | Design consequence — our inference |
|---|---|---|
| [Karnik et al., When Reasoning Helps Action, arXiv:2610.00601v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.00601v1), 30 September 2026; preprint | Separates correctability from actionability and compares steering with a compute-matched Best-of-4. Table II distinguishes the full 888-scene set from a 300-scene baseline-defined challenging subset; the latter's collision improvement coexists with increased close encounters. Manipulation reasoning improvements leave task performance largely unchanged. | Measure changed outcomes, adverse effects and full populations. A better signal, selected subset or aggregate score cannot by itself establish useful intervention. These different models and tasks are not a Sentinel comparator result. |
| [NVIDIA, Alpamayo 2 Super technical article](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/generate-trajectories-reasoning-traces-and-auto-labels-with-nvidia-alpamayo-2-super), 4 August 2026 | Distinguishes open-loop trajectory/reasoning evaluation from closed-loop execution, where actions alter later observations and, with reactive behavior models, other agents' behavior. | Separate our offline source-predicate witnesses from executed recovery. Record what remains fixed or reactive in each simulator; no equivalence to its benchmark or model is claimed. |
| [Waymo, Reference Driver introduction](https://community.waymo.com/blog/2026/06/reference-driver/), 10 June 2026 | Describes ReD as a closed-loop reference for belief updating, uncertainty and evasive action, extending reaction-timing models. | A responsive baseline must have meaningful action choices and a declared information/time contract. A weak brake-only comparator cannot establish broad superiority. No ReD integration or matching result is claimed. |

These sources reinforce the existing mission rather than justify a model upgrade.
Sentinel's current advantage is inspectable failure decomposition: exact retained
inputs, separate source-model and native outcomes, independent scalar checks,
finite witnesses, necessary constraints and explicit unresolved misses. That is
useful research infrastructure; a competitive advantage over frontier systems is
not established by it. The four repeatedly exposed development cases are a
qualification set, not an unbiased generalization benchmark.

The [branch-retention proposal](SENTINEL_BRANCH_RETENTION_PROPOSAL_2026-10-16.md)
is justified by the admitted discarded opportunity, with prospective online
selection and unchanged budgets. Qualify that interface once and report losses.
Beyond development qualification, reserve new scenes before tuning; measure
actual-history recovery and delay, benign intervention cost and joint misses;
then test one information ablation with matched actions and resources. Evidence
from those stages, rather than implementation complexity or publication recency,
will determine whether this direction earns a stronger claim.
